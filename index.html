Block Yard 3D

★ 0
Erase anything, even the ground, to carve rooms and dig ponds. Water pours downhill into your holes. Grow wheat and cotton, keep animals, and keep Tom and Mira happy. Tap any block to replace it. Chop the forest for wood and sell goods for coins. Use the view buttons to rotate and tilt the 3D camera. Give the villager a sealed room with a 2-tall door and place a Bed inside. He moves in: out by day, asleep in his bed at night.
